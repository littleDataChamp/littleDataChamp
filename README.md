### 🕵️‍♂️ About Me

I am a Data Scientist and AI Engineer based in Mumbai, specializing in decentralized architectures, open-weight models, and real-world business automation. When I am not leading technical discovery and AWS cloud migrations at **Zaptoz Technologies**, I am building **SONAR**—my firm dedicated to AI automation for the FMCG and wholesale sectors. 

* 🌍 **Community:** Founder and lead mentor of the **TRIDENT** community.
* 🧠 **Philosophy:** Privacy-first, agent-driven ecosystems over static dashboards.
* ⚡ **Currently Building:** *Daksh* (AI job recommendation engine) & *KHODIX* (Heavy machinery digital marketplace).
