### 👨‍💻 The Architecture Behind The Code

I am a Data Scientist and AI Engineer based in Mumbai, specializing in decentralized architectures, open-weight models, and real-world business automation. When I am not leading technical discovery and AWS cloud migrations at an **AWS Partner**, I am building **SONAR**—my firm dedicated to AI automation for the FMCG and wholesale sectors.

> **Current Protocol:** Engineering *Daksh* (AI job recommendation engine) & *KHODIX* (Heavy machinery digital marketplace).
> 
> **System Philosophy:** Privacy-first, agent-driven ecosystems over static dashboards.
> 
> **Community Node:** Founder and lead mentor of the **TRIDENT** community.
